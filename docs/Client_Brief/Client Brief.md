# Client Brief: Learner Support Portal
* Client: SkillsTrack Training Centre
* Project: Learner Support Portal


##  Background

* SkillsTrack Training Centre supports learners enrolled in short occupational programmes. Currently, learners record their goals, tasks, support bookings, and progress in separate documents and messages. This fragmented approach makes it difficult for assessors to track outstanding work, identify learners needing support, and monitor overall progress.


##  Objectives

* Provide a single, browser-based portal for learners and assessors.
* Improve visibility of learner progress and outstanding tasks.
* Streamline support booking and resource access.
* Enhance learner engagement with an integrated coding game.

##  Key Requirements

### Learners must be able to:
* Record and manage learning goals and tasks.
* Book support sessions.
* Track progress visually.
* Access learning resources.
* Play a short coding game.

### Assessors must be able to:
* View learner progress dashboards.
* Identify outstanding tasks.
* See which learners require support.


##  Technical Specifications

* Use JavaScript for building the app.
* Use Firebase for login, database, and hosting.
* Must work in a browser (desktop and mobile).
* Secure login and data protection.


##  Deliverables

* A working learner support portal.
* Simple user guide for learners and assessors.
* Deployed online with Firebase Hosting.
* Updates and fixes after launch.


##  Success Criteria

* Learners manage all activities in one place.
* Assessors can easily track progress and support needs.
* Portal is secure, reliable, and easy to use.
